# Marketing site (Persuade)

Scope: the multi-page public site (Home, Product, Nemotron, Pricing, sign-in) for Holdfast. The
workspace (Operate) is restyled afterwards in the same world.
Audience: customer-success and RevOps leads at B2B SaaS companies, plus hackathon judges who must
see how NVIDIA Nemotron is used. Action: start free (sign up, then the one-click synthetic demo).
Proof: the live product, synthetic demo data (labelled), real verification and holdout mechanics.
No logos, testimonials, customer counts or benchmark claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Holdfast's site is a triage board. Every morning's accounts arrive tagged, sequenced and
explained, ready to call. It refuses the category default: a dark gradient hero, a floating
glowing screenshot, a logo strip and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Ground:** a deep surgical-teal field (#0C4A47 to #0F5E59). Two more surfaces belong to the
  same world:
  - the darkest field step (#052523) for the proof section, the checks section and the footer;
  - tag stock (#F5F7F4) for long reading passages, so dense body copy keeps strong contrast (an
    accessibility need: WCAG AA body text) and the page alternates dense and quiet passages.
- **Tags:** tag-stock white (#F5F7F4) with punched grommets, wire loops and dashed perforations,
  tilted in real 3D.
- **Triage bars:** red #D7262E (call first), amber #F2B91F (watch), and slate #7C8A86 for lower;
  never green.
- **Type:** Archivo, with condensed widths for stamped headings and the regular width for text,
  plus Martian Mono for rank stamps and measured numbers.

STORY: A CS lead sees accounts arrive triaged, with a red tag on top. They understand the
mechanism: the engine measures, Nemotron writes the checked note, and a holdout proves the result.
They believe it because the numbers are checked and the demo is real. They act: start free and run
the demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- **Ground:** the teal field. A clipboard-rail nav sits on top.
- **Left five columns:** the headline at display scale, the subline, the primary "Start free" and
  a secondary "How Nemotron works".
- **Right seven columns:** one large 3D triage tag hangs from a wire and tilts with the pointer.
  It shows the #1 account (synthetic), its red bar, what stands out, a checked Nemotron note, a
  playbook citation and a perforated tear-off strip.
- **Bottom edge:** a moving strip of small tags.

FORM: the Triage Board, #5 of 7 on my ordered list; seed:25de086d366373da. Signature
interactions:
- tearing the hero tag's strip unfolds Nemotron's brief beneath it (raised from the Miura fold);
- attract loops in every section (from the arcade);
- dense real tag data (from the Designers Republic sleeve).
